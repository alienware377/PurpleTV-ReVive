Open source Xposed module for Twitch on Android

PurpleTV ReVive

7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ emotes, third-party badges, emote autocomplete and a pile of chat tweaks for the official Twitch app. A continuation of the great work of PurpleTV and bttv-android, for today's Twitch.

Download the latest release Source code on GitHub

What it adds

7TV, BTTV, FFZ and Homies emotes

Global and channel emotes render right in chat, animated ones included, with each channel's own pictures.

Emote autocomplete

Start typing a name, no colon needed. Pin your go-to emotes to the top, per streamer if you like.

Favorites bar

Long-press any emote to keep it in a bar at the top of the emote picker.

Third-party badges and pronouns

FFZ, BTTV, Chatterino, DankChat, Chatsen and Homies badges, plus pronouns next to names.

Deleted messages and timestamps

See what a deleted message said, and timestamps with seconds, even with Twitch's own turned off.

Highlights and blacklist

Tint lines with a word or from a user, and hide matching messages completely.

Video filters

24 filters for lives and VODs, from exposure and colour to gradient maps, grain and pixelate, each limited to the tones you pick.

Overlays and textures

Grunge, film grain, manga screentones, light leaks or your own picture, blended over the video with Photoshop's blend modes.

Emote picker sections

This channel's and the global 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes right inside Twitch's emote picker.

Player extras

Brightness and volume swipes, a sleep timer, and skipping the external link warning.

Notifications that work

Go-live alerts work even on the patched, ready-to-install APK, which normally cannot register for them.

Tidy up

Hide the hype train, leaderboards, chat header, Bits button, create button and more, one switch each.

What it will never do

No ad blocking, and no money-related mods of any kind.

Ads and subscriptions are how Twitch and the streamers on it get paid. This module is about the chat reading experience, not about taking anything away from creators.

Looking for PurpleTV or bttv-android?

Both brought third-party emotes to Twitch on Android, and bttv-android has stopped updating. PurpleTV ReVive picks up their chat features for today's Twitch app. It works as a module for the official app, so your Twitch login stays as it is. One difference to know: unlike PurpleTV, it has no ad blocking.

How to install

Easiest: ready-to-install APK

  1. From the latest release, download the Twitch file whose name ends in patched.apk.
  2. Uninstall the Play Store Twitch app, then install the downloaded one.
  3. Sign in with your password, or the Google, Amazon or Apple buttons.

Rooted: LSPosed

  1. Install the module file from the latest release (its name ends in module-twitch31.1.2.apk).
  2. In LSPosed, enable PurpleTV ReVive and tick Twitch in its scope.
  3. Force-stop and reopen Twitch.

No root: LSPatch

  1. Open LSPatch (v1.2 or newer) and pick your installed Twitch app.
  2. Choose Integrated mode and add the module file as a module.
  3. Install the patched Twitch that LSPatch makes.

Settings live inside Twitch: open Twitch's Settings, and PurpleTV is the first row. Android 8.0 or newer. Built for Twitch 31.1.2; Twitch 31.5 and newer changed how chat works, so chat features will likely break there until an update.

Questions

Do I need root?

No. Use LSPatch or the ready-to-install APK without root, or LSPosed with root.

Does it block ads?

No, and it never will. Ads pay Twitch and the streamers.

Why doesn't my passkey work?

A passkey is tied to the app's signing certificate, and a patched build is signed with a different key. Use your password, or the Google, Amazon or Apple buttons.

Can I get the normal Twitch app back?

Yes. Switch off "PurpleTV enabled" at the top of its settings and restart Twitch.

Is this allowed by Twitch?

Any client modification is against Twitch's Terms of Service, so use it at your own risk. It never automates chatting or viewing.
